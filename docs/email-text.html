Pick-A-Shift · e-mail copy, as of 10 Sep 2026

What every e-mail now says

The six e-mails and the unsubscribe page, reworded to match the agency's PDF “Email Format PICK-A-Shift”, plus the “shift updated” e-mail added since. Each one shows its subject line and the full message as it arrives, filled in with the PDF's own example values.

  1. 1Registration receivedto the new user
  2. 2Account now activeto the new user
  3. 3Shift is liveto the store
  4. 4Shift bookedto the applicant
  5. 5Applicant approvedto the store
  6. 6Shift cancelledto the applicant
  7. 7Unsubscribe pageon the website
  8. 8Shift updatedto the store · not in the PDF

What changed across every e-mail

  • The copyright line is now © Pick-A-Shift. All rights reserved. The year is gone.
  • The support address is always team@pickashift.ca. It used to come from the site settings, which held an old reliefshifts.com address.
  • The closing line is “If you have any questions, you may reach out to our support team at team@pickashift.ca.” The cancellation (6) leaves it out because it names the address in its own paragraph.
  • The footer still ends with “You are receiving this because you have an account on Pick-A-Shift.” and the Unsubscribe from these e-mails link.

Tinted text is filled in from the account or the shift. The example values are the ones in the PDF. Grey italic notes mark lines that only appear in some e-mails.
